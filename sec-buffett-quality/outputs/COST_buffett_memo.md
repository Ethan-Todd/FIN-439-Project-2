# COST Buffett-Style Business-Quality Memo

## Verdict: Own the business

Costco's reported record looks like a business owner would want to see. Revenue increased from **226,954.0** in the fiscal year ended **2022-08-28** to **303,154.0** in the fiscal year ended **2026-08-30**, while net income increased from **5,844.0** to **9,226.0**. The result is not built on accounting earnings alone: owner earnings increased from **3,501.0** to **9,382.0**, and they were positive in every year shown.

Returns on year-end equity remained above the coded hurdle in every year, ranging from **25.11%** to **31.19%**. Liabilities relative to equity also declined from **2.11x** to **1.49x**. Each coded check passed: ROE met the required level every year, owner earnings stayed positive, and owner earnings grew from the first year to the last.

I would classify this as a business worth owning on the evidence provided. That is a judgment about operating quality, not a recommendation to buy the stock: no price data was used, so this memo makes no conclusion about valuation or margin of safety.

# Decision memo — Project 2

**Recommendation: pilot the process; do not adopt it for client trading.** Use 85% SGOV / 15% VOO (0% IEF/LQD) as the reference paper allocation. The working mandate seeks minimum estimated volatility with at least 5% estimated annual return and 10% SGOV, using a long-only five-point grid. It describes a couple with roughly 15 years to retirement. The original working document does not supply a benchmark, numerical volatility ceiling, or cost budget. The user now selects 100% SGOV as a proposed fixed capital-preservation benchmark; this is a current selection, not an original Lab 13 requirement. No numerical risk ceiling or cost budget is inferred from Meridian.

At zero additional cost, the reference historical estimate is **5.2843% return and 2.4519% volatility**. Equal weight estimates 3.8242% return / 7.4726% volatility; frozen inverse volatility estimates 3.4920% / 1.3427%. Both comparisons satisfy the reserve minimum but fail the 5% return objective. Their weights remain fixed when optimization controls change. The separate 100% SGOV policy benchmark estimates **3.7151% annual return**, 1.2849 percentage points below the 5% objective, and therefore **fails** that rule. Its 100% SGOV reserve passes. Selecting a defensive comparison does not guarantee capital preservation or make the benchmark mandate-feasible.

The proposed **paper-pilot allocation range is 75–95% SGOV and the complementary 25–5% VOO**, with IEF/LQD at zero. This is the observed envelope of feasible selected portfolios in the disclosed routine stresses, not a confidence interval, suitability claim, or permission to trade anywhere in that range. The reference remains 85/15. Final student/client approval of the range is missing. The broad envelope describes the instability: it does not relax the five-point stability limit or turn a failing process into a passing one.

The window challenge is material: using 36 instead of 60 monthly returns selects 95% SGOV / 5% VOO, a 10-point movement. The mandate permits adoption only when a refreshed or alternative-window estimate does not move an allocation by more than five points; **that condition currently fails**. A one-percentage-point return reduction or 100 bp annual cost deduction selects 75/25 and makes the original allocation fail its return objective. At 25 bp additional annual cost the original allocation still estimates 5.0343%. Requiring 100% SGOV is infeasible at the 5% target. These are scenario settings, not new client constraints.

The proposed reserve-yield monitor compares the cutoff-vintage DGS3MO yield with the sample SGOV return estimate. At the **October 31, 2026 calendar-date cutoff**, the retained ALFRED response's latest observation is **October 29 at 4.18%**; the SGOV estimate is 3.7151%, so no yield-shortfall flag occurs. If the vintage yield falls below the SGOV estimate, re-estimate that sleeve and rerun feasibility before adoption; never substitute yield mechanically for total return or trade automatically. Missing or invalid archive evidence quarantines the signal. These responses were retrieved on November 16, not captured on October 31; they do not prove intraday availability or a historically executed decision.

**Monitoring and reversal:** halt allocation selection for invalid data/risk inputs or no feasible mix. Keep the process in pilot status while window sensitivity exceeds five points. Review when the reference portfolio fails a plausible cost/estimate stress, the reserve-yield monitor flags a shortfall, or its source evidence fails. Refresh before an actual future decision; the frozen October signal is not live advice. Review annually as the mandate specifies, and before any proposed adoption.

**Evidence needed before adoption:** retain a dated refreshed dataset, input assumptions, and a reproducible reference-versus-reasonable-alternative-window comparison. Both selected portfolios must remain feasible under the working mandate and no asset weight may differ by more than five percentage points. The existing 60/36-month comparison must be reported, not discarded or replaced solely to obtain a pass. Document cost assumptions, stress outcomes, valid signal evidence at the new decision cutoff, and any unresolved review flags. Passing the stability check is necessary under the mandate, not sufficient proof of suitability; final student/client review and authorization are still required.

**Committee ask:** authorize a paper evaluation only, acknowledge the proposed 100% SGOV comparison, and resolve the cost assumption before considering adoption. No numerical loss guarantee or volatility ceiling is asserted. The final allocation decision belongs to the student/client.
